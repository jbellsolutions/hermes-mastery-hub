# Module 09 — Capstone: your operating system

## Outcome
Your roles, workflows, and automations are assembled into a daily/weekly rhythm you actually
run — and you have a written 30-day plan.

## Required reading
- ../agent-feed/ASSESSMENT.md
- ../agent-feed/DAILY_LEARNING_LOG.md
- ../reference/11-best-practices.md

## Exercise
1. **Assemble the rhythm.** Decide what runs when:
   - Daily: operator standup (cron), inbox triage (email-reply).
   - Weekly: business review (founder-chief-of-staff), campaign (marketer), finance summary.
2. **Wire the recurring ones** as cron jobs delivering to your gateway (only the proven ones).
3. **Self-assess:** fill `../agent-feed/ASSESSMENT.md` — honestly mark your level.
4. **Write your 30-day plan** into `../agent-feed/DAILY_LEARNING_LOG.md`: which roles to add,
   which workflows to automate, which skills to author next.
5. **Lock in the compounding habit:** every time you do something twice, make it a skill.

## Artifact
- A written operating rhythm + a filled assessment + a 30-day plan in the learning log.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] You can run your business day through the agent (with approvals)
- [ ] At least one recurring job runs unattended and reaches you
- [ ] ASSESSMENT.md filled; you know your level
- [ ] 30-day plan written with concrete next skills/workflows

## The honest finish line
You now have the **operating system and habits of a top-tier Hermes user**: a working setup, a
chat-native gateway, approval-gated business + personal workflows, custom skills, and
automation. That's the "top 1%" *operating posture*. Genuine mastery is the next 30–90 days of
running it daily while the agent's memory and skills compound around your specific life and
business. Keep the learning log. Keep making skills. That's the whole game.

## Next action
Run it for real tomorrow. Add one role or automation from your 30-day plan.
